privacy

What this site collects.

Short version: the inquiry form, and nothing else.

The inquiry form

When you send an inquiry, the answers you type are delivered to me by email and stored by my form provider so there's a record if an email goes missing. I use them to reply to you and to scope the work. I don't sell them, share them, or add you to a mailing list.

Analytics

If analytics are enabled on this site, they record aggregate page views and referrers. They are not used to build a profile of you.

Cookies

This site sets no tracking cookies. Your light or dark theme choice is stored in your own browser's local storage and never leaves your device.

Client work shown here

The screenshots in the gallery are of work I built. Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners and appear here to identify the work, not to imply endorsement.

Deleting your data

Email balmacedamarvin@gmail.com and I'll delete your inquiry and confirm it's gone.
