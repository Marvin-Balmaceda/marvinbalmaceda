before you ask

The questions that come up first.

How do you price a project?

Every project is a fixed fee against a written scope, quoted after one short conversation. You know the number before anything starts. If the scope changes I send a change note with a new number before I build it — you will never get a surprise invoice.

How long does a typical project take?

A landing page or order form is usually days. A full brand site is three to six weeks depending on how settled the design is. A membership or course build runs longer, because the testing matters more than the building.

Do you work with my existing designer?

Often. A large share of my work is building someone else's design faithfully — Figma, XD or Photoshop. I flag the places a design will fight the platform before I start, not after.

What if my site is already built?

Then it's a rebuild, a rescue or a care plan. I audit what's there first and tell you which of the three it actually is, even when that's the smaller job.

Do you work with US timezones?

Yes. I'm in Manila, UTC+8, and I hold a daily overlap with US teams. In ten years the timezone has not been the constraint.

What happens after launch?

Either I hand over documented and you take it from there, or I stay on a flat monthly retainer for updates, backups, speed and tracking. Both are fine. Neither is automatic.
